Case Study C: “One-Size Prayer”

Theme: Cultural Competence

Format: 4-voice — spoken dialogue + inner monologue. Assign four readers or two readers doubling inner voices.

MARY — white Protestant chaplain, 68, lifelong church elder — warm, softly spoken

LINDA — Asian-American woman, 70, longtime Beacon member & practicing Buddhist, grieving the recent death of her childhood friend Mei

Scene

MARY (spoken, entering softly): Good afternoon, Linda. I’ve kept you close in prayer since Mei passed. How is your spirit today?
MARY (inner): Be present, acknowledge the loss.
LINDA (spoken, voice thin): The waves of sorrow still come. Breathing and chanting steady me.
LINDA (inner): I hope she remembers I’m Buddhist.
MARY (spoken, settling): Breathing sounds healing. May I first offer a brief blessing that comforts me in times of grief?
MARY (inner): Share without imposing.
LINDA (spoken): Yes — silence afterward would help.
LINDA (inner): Space to breathe after, good.
MARY (spoken, soft): “May peace rest upon you, and may Mei rejoice now in heaven’s gentle embrace, watched over by the Holy One.”
MARY (inner): A picture of hope beyond; surely universal?
LINDA (inner): Heaven? In my tradition we speak of rebirth, not heaven. Accept her kindness, but clarify.
MARY (spoken): Let’s share three breaths of silence for Mei.

Both breathe together slowly.

LINDA (spoken, gently): Mary, thank you. In Buddhism we imagine loved ones journeying onward toward awakening. Your blessing was kind, though the word heaven lands differently for me.
LINDA (inner): Offer teaching without scolding.
MARY (spoken, warm but reflective): I appreciate you telling me. I’m sorry — I slipped into my own language. Pluralism is a practice, isn’t it?
MARY (inner): Humility, learn.
LINDA (spoken): Exactly. I value that our traditions can sit side by side, even in grief. Thank you for the peace your words carried; may I share a loving-kindness verse for Mei and for us?
LINDA (inner): Reciprocity, invitation.
MARY (spoken, nodding): Please — I’d be honored. Next visit I can bring a meditation bell, if that feels supportive.
MARY (inner): Adapt to her practice.
LINDA (spoken, softly): That would be wonderful.
LINDA (inner): We’re learning together — this is pluralism alive.

Discussion Prompts

  • What did you notice about the spiritual language used in this conversation?
  • How might religious differences impact pastoral care?
  • What skills does Mary demonstrate when Linda gives her feedback?
  • How does the relationship between Mary and Linda shift during this conversation?
  • What might this conversation teach us about interfaith care?
  • Have you seen or experienced moments like this in your congregation?

After Discussion

  • Whole-group harvest: themes recorded
  • Facilitator analysis made and recorded
  • Each person writes at least one personal takeaway
