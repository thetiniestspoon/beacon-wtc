Case Study D: “Presence Over Apps”

Theme: Intergenerational Care + Pronoun Tension

Format: 4-voice — spoken dialogue + inner monologue. Assign four readers or two readers doubling inner voices.

AVERY — chaplain, non-binary, 27, uses they/them; earnest problem-solver

MRS. GREEN — congregant, woman, 82, small pension, limited mobility, shrinking social circle

Scene

AVERY (spoken, bright): Mrs. Green, lovely to see you! Have you been able to make it to Sunday service?
AVERY (inner): Maybe a neighbour’s giving her lifts.
MRS. GREEN (spoken, sighs): Not anymore, dear. No licence, and Doris’s hip won’t let her drive far now.
MRS. GREEN (inner): Another friend edged out by time.
AVERY (spoken): I could match you with another driver in the congregation — ride share?
AVERY (inner): Simple fix.
MRS. GREEN (spoken, hesitant): Most folks my age have moved or passed. I hate to ask young families to detour.
MRS. GREEN (inner): My circle’s a dwindling ring.
AVERY (spoken, upbeat pivot): How about our livestream? Do you watch the service online?
AVERY (inner): Tech bridge!
MRS. GREEN (spoken): I do click in sometimes. But it’s lonely humming to a screen. I sang in the choir for thirty-two years — nothing matches voices all around you.
MRS. GREEN (inner): I miss harmony, not video.
AVERY (inner): Okay, livestream isn’t enough. What else?
AVERY (spoken): Maybe we could fund a taxi once a month? We could crowd-source the fare.
AVERY (inner): Money gets her there.
MRS. GREEN (spoken, gentle): Taxi tips pile up, love, and I’d still sit alone at coffee hour — my pew pals are gone.
MRS. GREEN (inner): Heaping bills, thin company.
AVERY (inner): She’s shutting every door.
AVERY (spoken, strained smile): I’ll keep brainstorming. There must be a way.
AVERY (inner): Why does she sound so Eeyore?
MRS. GREEN (spoken, soft): You’re a kind young girl. It helps just knowing you care. Do you know “Blue Boat Home”? It was my solo once.
MRS. GREEN (inner): Maybe music will spark connection.
AVERY (inner): Girl again — let it slide.
AVERY (spoken): I love that hymn. Beautiful imagery.
AVERY (inner): At least we found common ground. Still, no solid solution.
MRS. GREEN (inner): If only this kind child understood I need people, not plans.

Discussion Prompts

  • What is Avery trying to accomplish in this conversation?
  • What is Mrs. Green looking for in this interaction?
  • How do generational differences affect communication here?
  • What assumptions about needs and solutions appear in this conversation?
  • What might be missing from Avery’s approach to helping?
  • Have you seen or experienced moments like this in your congregation?

After Discussion

  • Whole-group harvest: themes recorded
  • Facilitator analysis made and recorded
  • Each person writes at least one personal takeaway
