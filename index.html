Widening the Circle

Case Studies for Culturally Competent Leaders

A training resource from the Beacon Unitarian Universalist Congregation

About These Case Studies

These scenarios are designed for small-group discussion in chaplain training, small group ministry, and congregational development settings. Each scene presents a realistic encounter that surfaces dynamics around identity, assumptions, care, and connection.

The original four case studies (A–D) use a 4-voice format with spoken dialogue and inner monologue, inviting readers to see both what is said and what is felt. The two new case studies (E–F) use dialogue and narration only, focusing on what is spoken aloud.

All scenarios are aligned to the eight principles of the Unitarian Universalist Association.

How to Use

  • Assign readers and read the scenario aloud together
  • Use the reflection questions for individual and group discussion
  • Facilitator guidance provides structure for a 20-minute group session
  • Print-friendly: use your browser’s print function (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P) for clean handouts

Reading Formats

Cases A–D: Four Voices

Each scene includes spoken dialogue and inner monologue (shown in italics). Assign four readers at your table, or two readers who double the inner voices.

Cases E–F: Three Voices

These scenes use spoken dialogue only plus narration. Assign three readers: one for each character and one for stage directions.
