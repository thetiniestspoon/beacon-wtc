Case Study B: “Resource Rescue”

Theme: Savior Complex

Format: 4-voice — spoken dialogue + inner monologue. Assign four readers or two readers doubling inner voices.

MOLLY — white woman, 46, chaplain

DARIUS — Black man, 35, congregant

Scene

MOLLY (spoken, upbeat on phone): Darius! I couldn’t stop thinking about your job search and put together a surprise plan.
MOLLY (inner): He’ll be so grateful I leapt into action.
DARIUS (spoken, guarded): Oh? Thanks for calling.
DARIUS (inner): I asked for prayer, not a project manager…
MOLLY (spoken): Good news first: my friend at CareerWorks squeezed you into a résumé clinic Thursday at 9 a.m.!
MOLLY (inner): Networks are power — share them!
DARIUS (spoken): Thursday morning — I have the kids.
DARIUS (inner): No childcare, no discussion.
MOLLY (spoken, rapid): Already covered! Tess from church offered to babysit. She’ll arrive 8:30.
MOLLY (inner): See? One step ahead.
DARIUS (spoken, slower): Molly, that’s generous, yet it feels a bit fast. I’m trying to process everything.
DARIUS (inner): Control is slipping from my hands.
MOLLY (spoken, stumbling): Oh — goodness, I — I’m so sorry! I just wanted to help. I probably overstepped. I feel awful now.
MOLLY (inner): Ugh, Molly, you messed up. He must think you’re a meddler.
DARIUS (spoken, gentle): It’s okay. Maybe we can start with a conversation about what support looks like for me?
DARIUS (inner): Can she stay with my feelings, not hers?
MOLLY (spoken, voice quivers): Yes, of course. I’m really embarrassed — I hate that I made this about me.
MOLLY (inner): Center him, Molly. Deep breath.
DARIUS (spoken): How about we pray together, then brainstorm next steps when I’m ready?
DARIUS (inner): Partnership, not rescue.
MOLLY (spoken, calmer): Absolutely. I’m listening.
MOLLY (inner): Step back, hold space.

Discussion Prompts

  • How would you describe the dynamic between Molly and Darius?
  • What is Darius feeling as the conversation progresses?
  • How does the conversation shift partway through? What causes this shift?
  • What different approaches to helping are at play here?
  • Have you seen or experienced moments like this in your congregation?

After Discussion

  • Whole-group harvest: themes recorded
  • Facilitator analysis made and recorded
  • Each person writes at least one personal takeaway
